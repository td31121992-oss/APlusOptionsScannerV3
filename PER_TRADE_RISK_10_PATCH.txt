APlusOptionsScannerV3 - Full Session + Per-Trade Risk 10% Patch
================================================================

Risk rule
---------
For BUY CE / BUY PE paper trades, capital required for the trade is the total
option premium committed:

    trade_capital = option_entry_price * quantity
    maximum_trade_risk = trade_capital * 10%

Examples:
    Rs 30,000 trade capital -> Rs 3,000 max trade risk
    Rs 24,750 trade capital -> Rs 2,475 max trade risk

The default long-option stop-loss is also changed from 25% to 10%, so the
selected option's planned loss matches the per-trade capital risk budget.

This replaces the old rule that calculated per-trade risk as 0.5% of total
account capital. Account capital is still used for separate session-level
safeguards such as daily-loss and total-premium limits.

Full-session paper trading
--------------------------
This package also includes the corrected full-session scanner:
- paper CE/PE signals can be generated throughout 09:15-15:30 when quality gates pass
- no 14:45/15:05 paper-trade cutoff
- live orders remain disabled
- paper trade journal persists generated signals

New/updated environment variables
---------------------------------
APLUS_SAFETY_TRADE_RISK_PERCENT=10.0
APLUS_OPTION_STOP_LOSS_PERCENT=10.0

The installer does not edit .env. Defaults are already 10%, so these lines are
optional unless you want to override them later.

Validation
----------
The installer compiles the corrected modules and runs:
- safety_gate_self_test.py
- trade_risk_self_test.py
- continuous_intraday_self_test.py

After installation restart the scanner:
    run_intraday_movement.bat

Live orders remain disabled.
