APlus Options Scanner V3 - Intraday Metrics Patch
====================================================

Purpose
-------
Broker portals commonly show percentage change from the previous market close.
That mixes overnight gap and today's actual move.

This patch separates:

1. Previous-close change:
   (LTP - previous close) / previous close * 100

2. Gap:
   (09:15/day open - previous close) / previous close * 100

3. Actual intraday move:
   (LTP - 09:15 open) / 09:15 open * 100

4. Open-to-high move:
   (day high - 09:15 open) / 09:15 open * 100

5. Distance below high:
   (day high - LTP) / day high * 100

6. Day-range position:
   (LTP - day low) / (day high - day low) * 100

7. Trend retention:
   Percentage of the maximum directional move from open that remains at LTP.

8. Opening-range distance:
   Directional percentage beyond the 09:15-09:30 range.

The radar score now gives much greater weight to the actual move from today's
open. A large overnight gap with no follow-through will not rank like a genuine
opening drive.

This patch does not enable live order placement.
