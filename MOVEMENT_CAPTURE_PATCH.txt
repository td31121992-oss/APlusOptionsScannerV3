APlus Options Scanner V3 - Movement Capture Patch
====================================================

This patch keeps the existing Trade Quality Score and adds a separate
Movement Capture Score.

Movement Capture Score weights
------------------------------
Actual move from 09:15 open:       35
Relative opening volume:           20
Directional trend retention:       20
Position near directional edge:    15
Opening-range expansion:           10
Total:                             100

New operational shortlists
--------------------------
1. EARLY_ENTRY_CANDIDATES
   - The existing entry, structure, VWAP, volume, risk and timing rules pass.
   - Only this list may proceed to option-chain selection.

2. STRONG_TREND_WAIT_FOR_PULLBACK
   - Strong directional movement is already visible.
   - A fresh entry is unavailable, late or unsuitable at the current price.
   - These stocks must not be chased.
   - Watch for a controlled pullback toward VWAP/EMA structure and a new
     continuation trigger.
